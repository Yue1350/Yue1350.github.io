Terms of Service

These Terms of Service govern your use of TTS Bot (hereinafter referred to as "the Bot"), a Discord bot providing text-to-speech and voice channel utilities.

1. Service Description

The Bot provides the following features within Discord servers:

  • Converting chat messages and common abbreviations into voice audio via Google Cloud TTS.
  • Announcing user joins/leaves in voice channels.
  • Handling custom emojis and resending them via Webhooks.
  • Managing per-guild settings such as voice selection, playback speed, and dedicated channels.
